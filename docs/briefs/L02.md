Mini-game "Stepping stones": a gap of water between two cliffs; 4-5 paper letters float down marked only "No" / "Not this time"
(no program names, ever). Drag (or tap) each letter onto the gap: it folds and turns into a flat stone that becomes a
stepping stone. When the path of stones is complete a small figure hops across and onWin() fires. ~25 s.
Hint: "Drag each letter into the water."  The mood is calm, not sad: the message is that every no became a step.
Diorama: two closed wooden gates on stone pillars, a few letters scattered; when unlocked the gates swing open and a
line of stepping stones appears across a little pond.
