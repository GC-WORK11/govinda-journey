This is the level that nods to Wispr Flow; make it delightful. Mini-game "Words that travel": a clay phone with a big soft
mic button and a laptop with an empty document + blinking cursor. Visitor holds the mic and SPEAKS (Web Speech API,
webkitSpeechRecognition, en-IN, interim results shown on the phone) OR, if speech is unsupported/denied, types into a
small text field on the phone. On release the sentence lifts off the phone as individual words that fly in an arc and
land at the laptop cursor, typed letter by letter. After 2 lines (any words, min 2 words each) -> onWin(). Suggest a line
softly ("try: hello from the other side of the room"). Never show raw errors from the speech API. ~30 s.
Hint: "Hold the mic and say a line, or type it."
Diorama: a desk with a laptop and a phone on a small stand, a tiny paper-word ribbon floating between them after unlock.
