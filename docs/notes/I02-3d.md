# I02 — On the road

## Game
Hold anywhere to drive a toy clay train around a visible oval track in the island's front play space. Release to coast. After one lap the train auto-brakes into the station platform (glowing pennant flag) and stops -> passengers pop up waving, whistle blip, chime, onWin.

## Verb
HOLD (useHoldAnywhere). Always winnable: hold ~4 s or keep holding — the train pulls into the station by itself on the last 0.9 rad.
