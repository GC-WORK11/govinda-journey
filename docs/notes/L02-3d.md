# L02 — Rejections (3D mini-game)

## The game
DRAG verb: Three cream-colored paper letter envelopes with terracotta wax seals float and bob near the traveller.
A shallow stream of water crosses the middle-front of the island with three glowing stepping-stone slots.
Drag a letter over the stream → nearest empty slot glows brighter. Drop on slot → letter squashes down into a
rounded stone (squash-stretch animation + water splash burst + blip sound). Wrong drop springs the letter home.
All three stones placed → warm chime → onWin() → gates open in payoff (handled by world).
