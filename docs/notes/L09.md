# L09 Chalk - Completion Notes

## Mini-game
A clay blackboard where a teacher message bubble asks a helper to "make a 3 question test on photosynthesis". The visitor taps "Send", and three question cards are drawn on the board with chalk-stroke animation. Each has 2-3 options. The visitor answers them (any answer accepted; correct answers get a chalk tick drawn as a stroke animation). When all three are answered, the school gate slides open → onWin().

Questions are factually correct and simple:
1. What do plants make during photosynthesis? → Oxygen
2. What do plants need from the sun? → Light  
3. Which part of the plant catches sunlight? → Leaves

## Diorama
Small Indian school building with cream walls + terracotta roof, courtyard with sand-colored ground, flag pole (no flag symbol), blackboard on the wall. Permanent scaffolding in one corner (because Chalk is still being built). After unlock: gate slides open, warm lamp glows at the gate (dusk feeling).
