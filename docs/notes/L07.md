# L07 - Wispr Wrap

## Mini-game
"Words that travel" - A delightful nod to Wispr Flow. Visitor speaks or types a line into a clay phone, and the words fly in an arc to land letter-by-letter on a laptop cursor. Complete 2 lines (min 2 words each) to win.

## Implementation
- **Speech mode**: Uses Web Speech API (webkitSpeechRecognition, en-IN) when available, with interim results shown on phone screen
- **Type mode**: Fallback when speech is unsupported/denied or in testing environments (detects HeadlessChrome, missing mediaDevices, or `?qa` param)
- **Word animation**: Words launch from phone with staggered timing (120ms apart), arc through the air over 1.2s, then type onto laptop screen
- **Win condition**: 2 complete lines, each with at least 2 words
