# L03 — First websites

Mini-game "Ship it by Sunday": drag (or tap-select, then tap slot) the header / hero / button
pieces into a paper browser window. Wrong slots wobble and spring the piece home. When all three
are in, a Deploy lever appears — pull it (a tap also works) and a paper plane flies off = shipped.
The four real sites (CEO Hunt, Flow Pitch, Workbench, Doorway) then hang for a beat as little shop
signs before onWin() fires. ~30 s.

Diorama: weekend market street — four stalls with coloured awnings + hanging painted sign boards,
cobble path, crate stack. One Scaffold cloth covers the row until unlock; after unlock the awnings
flutter and a small paper plane circles the stalls. ~60 meshes.
