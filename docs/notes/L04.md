# L04 "Alive" — Completion Notes

## What it is
Mini-game "Clear the air": A stylized top-down SVG map of Delhi streets covered in haze. Visitor taps 3 glowing spots to place air sensors. Each sensor colors nearby streets by air quality (soft green/amber/terracotta). After placing all 3 sensors, the haze lifts street by street over ~3.8 seconds and the game wins.

## Implementation
- **Game.tsx**: SVG mini-map (340×300) with 9 streets, 7 building blocks, and 1 park. Three sensor spots pulse with soft animation. Tapping a spot places a sensor and colors its assigned streets. After 3rd placement, timeline ticks every 580ms and haze opacity reduces from 0.72 to 0. Shows pills for sensor count, "240k places" detail, and "every 15 min" after all sensors placed. Hint: "Tap the bright spots to place sensors."

- **Diorama.tsx**: Greenhouse (glass-look soft blue panels, matte finish) at center-back with 4 small plants inside that sway when unlocked. Two Delhi rooftops (cream blocks) with flat terracotta roofs and small water tanks. Small India Gate-style arch (sand-colored with torus arch and dome). Three hazy grey puffs that float gently and disappear when unlocked.

- **L04.css**: Soft rounded shapes, matte cream/sand palette, gentle pulse/glow animations for sensor spots, responsive layout. Animations respect prefers-reduced-motion.

- **scripts/qa/L04.mjs**: Clicks sensor-0, sensor-1, sensor-2 in sequence with 400ms delays, then waits 4.5s for completion.

## Timing
Game completes in ~30 seconds:
- First sensor: ~3s to locate and tap
- Second sensor: ~4s 
- Third sensor: ~4s
- Haze clearing animation: 3.8s
- Total: ~15-20s for quick players, up to 30s for careful observation

## Style notes
Colors follow brief: soft matte palette (no neon), rounded shapes, gentle shadows. Streets use green/amber/terracotta not pure colors. Haze is soft grey (#999 at 48% opacity in puffs, 72% max in game overlay). All animations are smooth and clay-like.
