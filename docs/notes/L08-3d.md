# L08 — Sign Flow (3D)

Game: a clay laptop on a crate projects a glowing ghost hand sign; three big clay
hands float in front in different poses. Verb: TAP — "Tap the hand that matches".
Correct hand spins into the screen, a letter-tile pops onto the display, burst+blip.
Wrong hand wobbles "no" and stays. 3 rounds (peace, palm, point) → chime → onWin.
Payoff: ghost palm waves from the screen, sparkles, leftover hands settle.

Files: src/levels/L08/Play.tsx (parametric ClayHand from capsules, 4 poses),
scripts/qa/L08.mjs (finds l08-ghost-<pose>, taps l08-hand-<pose>, retries).
Diorama.tsx unchanged.

Robustness: each hand has a named invisible hit-sphere at the palm so taps land
anywhere near it; QA passed desktop+mobile, 0 console problems. tsc clean.

Rough: the ghost is small on a phone (it's on the laptop screen); readable but
could be a larger hologram if it ever feels cramped.
