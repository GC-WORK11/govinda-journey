# I01 — Stillness (3D mini-game)

**Game**: Breathing meditation with a lotus that opens as you hold.  
**Verb**: HOLD (useHoldAnywhere) — press and hold anywhere, then release.  
**Mechanic**: Hold for ~2.5s to fully open the lotus (one breath). Release when full to light a lamp. Three breaths → win.

**Files**:
- `src/levels/I01/Play.tsx` — lotus with 8 petals (2 layers), halo ring, 3 floating lamps, ripple effect
- `scripts/qa/I01.mjs` — three 2.8s holds with 800ms pauses
