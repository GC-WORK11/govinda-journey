# I01 — Stillness

**Mini-game.** A small clay pond drawn in the sheet. Hold the pond to breathe in: a ring of clay pebbles widens
over 4 s and the sheet brightens softly; let go to breathe out and the ring settles back. Three breaths finish it
(~20 s). A hold under 2.5 s does not count, but after three short tries the next one is accepted, so nobody gets
stuck. Guide text moves between "Breathe in" / "Breathe out" (and "Let go" once the lungs are full), with three
pebbles under the pond filling in as breaths land.

**Diorama.** A calm pond with lily pads, a flat meditation stone with a folded cloth, reeds and a small tree. The
stone is under the shared `Scaffold` until the level is solved; after unlock slow rings spread over the water and
seven small warm fireflies drift low over it.

**Verified.**
- `npx tsc -p . --noEmit` clean.
- `node scripts/level-check.mjs I01` -> PASS on desktop (1440x900) and mobile (390x844), QA script WON, 0 console
  problems, screenshots reviewed (game at rest, mid-breath, after, island).
- `scripts/qa/I01.mjs` wins with real pointer holds only (4.15 s down, 3.2 s up-wait, x3) and retries a breath if a
  hot reload interrupts one.

**Rough.**
- The sheet scene is SVG illustration, so it is flatter than the 3D diorama; it stays in the clay palette.
- Breath timing uses wall-clock timestamps rather than frame deltas, because the shared box drops frames under load
  and delta accumulation made a 4 s hold register as ~2 s (this made the first QA runs flaky).
- Fireflies are deliberately tiny and low-opacity; they read at island scale but not from far away.
