# L08 — Sign Flow

Mini-game: "Teach a sign". Step 1, visitor taps one of three clay hand cards (open palm, two fingers,
fist — all SVG silhouettes) then taps the action it should do (“hello”, Space, Enter; one-to-one).
Step 2, the laptop doc asks for "hello", Space, Enter in order; tapping the right hand plays a
21-point dot-landmark skeleton trace on it and types the key into the doc. 3 correct -> onWin,
wrong -> gentle shake. "Use my camera" link shows the getUserMedia feed in a rounded frame if
granted, hides itself cleanly if denied/unavailable.

Diorama: open-air studio on a small wooden deck — big terracotta clay hand on a stone plinth,
phone on a wooden tripod filming it, stool with spare clay. Scaffold covers the sculpture until
win; after unlock the hand slowly opens and closes (jointed fingers) and the phone's record dot
pulses.
