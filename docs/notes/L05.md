# L05 — Ent

**Mini-game "Fit the model":** a stack of 12 layer slabs (2–4 GB each, 30 GB total) is split by three
draggable clay pins into four contiguous slices, one per laptop (16 / 8 / 8 / 4 GB, OS labels
Linux · Windows · Mac · Linux). Overflowing slices tint soft red and the laptop wobbles; tapping a
gap also moves the nearest allowed pin (touch-friendly). When everything fits, screens glow and a
token hops laptop→laptop along dashed Wi-Fi arcs, spelling out "small machines think together" →
`onWin()`. "Auto-split" appears after 25 s and animates pins to a valid split (solutions are
(5,8,11), (6,8,11), (6,9,11) — pin 3 is forced to gap 11, so it's a real but kind puzzle).

**Diorama:** four clay laptops on a round wooden table under the big Ent tree, a tiny router behind
them, cables gathering at the leg and fanning across the soil like roots. On unlock the screens glow
(each in its group hue), the router LED blinks, and a word-bead hops laptop to laptop on a loop.
