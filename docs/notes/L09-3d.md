# L09 — Chalk (3D mini-game, polish pass)

**Game:** A standing chalkboard faces the camera in the open play space. It shows `3 + 4 =` in chalk with a faint chalk ring + `?` answer slot. Three chunky cream pebbles (6, 7, 8, ~0.6 units across) float in a row in front, bobbing with a warm pulsing glow.

**Verb:** Tap. Wrong pebble wobbles with a dust puff and settles back. Right pebble (7) arcs onto the board slot, a chalk tick draws itself under the question, bursts + chime fire, then onWin() — the Diorama gate/lamp handle the rest.

**Files:**
- src/levels/L09/Play.tsx — board (legs/tray/lantern/chalk sticks), pebbles, fly-to-slot anim all in useFrame refs
- scripts/qa/L09.mjs — clicks `__journey.find('L09-ans-7')` with a project() fallback

**Night readability:** pebbles use glowMat('#ffc98f') pulsing until touched; board face has slight emissive; a small clay lantern on the frame + one warm pointLight lights the play area. Question/`?`/tick/digits use unlit chalk-white materials so they read in the dark.
